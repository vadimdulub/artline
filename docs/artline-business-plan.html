Artline Strategy brief · September 2026

Monetization & promotion

Your advantage: art, books and history connected in one timeline, with museum collections to explore. Start with curious learners and museum visitors.

Recommended start

Attract visitors with visual history guides. Keep exploration free and earn from museum tickets and books. Test a paid study plan once people return regularly.

Ways to earn

  1. First

    Museum ticket referrals

    Add relevant ticket links to museum pages. Earn commission on referred bookings through a partner such as Tiqets. Start with five museums.

  2. Next

    Book referrals

    Link book records and reading lists to purchasable editions. Bookshop.org pays for referred purchases. Match the retailer to your audience’s country.

  3. Later

    Artline Plus

    Personal collections, museum wishlists and printable study guides. Test €5/month with frequent users. These are proposed features; visitor accounts and payments need building.

How to attract users

  • Useful guides for Google Search

    Start with “Monet in Paris,” “Byzantine art in museums” and “Books of the Renaissance.” Add original context and sources, following Google’s content guidance. Link each guide to its timeline.

  • Visual stories on social media

    Test Instagram carousels and Pinterest posts: one artwork, one book and one event from the same era. Invite people to explore the full story in Artline.

  • Teachers and history creators

    Offer a useful themed timeline to ten relevant teachers or creators. Ask for feedback and an optional share with their audience.

  • A weekly discovery email

    Invite visitors to subscribe for three connected discoveries each week. Link back to the atlas to encourage another visit.

The first 30 days

Publish three guides, share six visual posts, contact ten potential collaborators and add an email signup. Apply to affiliate programs and test relevant referral links after approval.

Measure: visits by channel, email signups, return visits and referral purchases. Put effort into the channel that brings returning users; consider paid promotion after that.

Proposed experiments, not a revenue forecast. The €5 price is a test. Partner approval, coverage and terms need confirmation. Disclose affiliate links and use images cleared for commercial reuse.
