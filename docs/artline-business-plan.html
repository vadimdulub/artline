Artline Strategy brief / revised 10 October 2026

Monetization & promotion

Your advantage: art, books and history connected in one timeline, with museum collections to explore. Start with curious learners and museum visitors.

Launch priority

Focus on launch and first users: one audience, two useful guides, targeted invitations and repeat visits. Follow the expanded 30-day launch plan for tasks, examples and measurement. The revenue ideas below are later experiments.

Revenue ideas to validate later

  1. Later test

    Museum ticket referrals

    If visitors use Artline for museum research, assess a ticket-referral partner’s current terms, coverage and eligibility. Start with a small relevant sample after acceptance. Keep documented holdings separate from current display.

  2. Later test

    Book referrals

    If reading lists attract repeat use, test links to correctly matched purchasable editions. Research retailer eligibility, current partner terms and audience geography before selecting a program.

  3. Later

    Artline Plus

    Interview returning readers about collections, notes and study exports. €5/month was an earlier price hypothesis, not a validated price. Accounts have since been implemented; paid concepts and checkout availability need feature-specific verification before promotion.

How to attract users

  • Useful guides for Google Search

    Start with “Monet in Paris,” “Byzantine art in museums” and “Books of the Renaissance.” Add original context and sources, following Google’s content guidance. Link each guide to its timeline.

  • Visual stories on social media

    Test one visual channel first: four posts and two short demos drawn from the two launch guides. Invite people to the exact guide discussed, and judge the channel by exploration and return visits.

  • Teachers and history creators

    Research 20 relevant educators, creators and learners; invite them in small batches. Offer a sourced comparison exercise and aim for five reader conversations. Sharing is optional.

  • A weekly discovery email

    Offer one artwork and one connection each week only after signup, delivery and unsubscribe work. Until then, use related guides to give readers a reason to return.

The first 30 days

Prepare two sourced guides, four visual posts and two short demos. Make up to 20 relevant personal invitations, contribute to two suitable community discussions and complete five reader sessions. Budget 40 founder hours; use the linked launch plan’s checklist.

Working goals: 100 qualified first-time visitors, 30 meaningful explorers and six returning readers from at least 20 eligible early explorers. These are proposed targets, not current results. Definitions and fallback measurement are in the launch plan.
