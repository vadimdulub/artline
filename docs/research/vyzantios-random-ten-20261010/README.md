# Periklis Vyzantios and ten random artists

**424 new review artworks, 242 new primary pictures and one existing artwork creator link** were delivered to production. Periklis Vyzantios now has **42 catalogue works and 15 pictures**, up from 29 works and three pictures. Across all 11 artists, the catalogue has 784 active works and 369 pictures.

## Coverage

All 800 linked entries in the 11 accessible WikiArt artist indexes were reconciled against the existing catalogue and English metadata lists. Seven explicitly post-1970 entries were excluded from new delivery; 127 source listings retain unresolved object or version identity. The other 666 listings support 417 new works and 249 existing-work evidence updates. Unknown dates remain explicit. Complete lifetime coverage is not established, and catalogue totals do not imply every work has a picture.

Official Vyzantios research adds 12 museum object references: nine National Gallery of Greece pages, one Teloglion drawing, one National Historical Museum painting and one existing Rhodes museum work. Four National Gallery pages duplicate confirmed WikiArt works; seven native works are new. The Rhodes record was linked to Vyzantios without replacing its Greek creator label. These references overlap, so source-listing counts are not unique artwork counts.

The report has 811 direct source picture links. Only selected, identity-reviewed reproductions with supported creation dates were attached. The Rhodes source page is retained without a new direct picture URL. Existing catalogue pictures and source references are also included in the catalogue view.

## Remaining source and image gaps

- **Vyzantios:** the National Gallery, Teloglion, Rhodes and National Historical Museum object evidence is retained. The National Historical Museum’s Hydra gallery also includes his son Konstantinos; its general exhibition text was not converted into invented object records. The Teloglion drawing has no verified matching institution authority yet. Eikastikon supplied corroborating reproductions and dimensions. Private collections and lifetime completeness remain unresolved.
- **Kittelsen:** 78 WikiArt listings remain identity holds, including alternate reproductions and versions. The [Kittelsen Museum collection](https://www.theodorkittelsen.no/blaafarvevaerkets-kittelsen-samling/) and Nasjonalmuseet are further collection sources; their full holdings have not been reconciled in this pass.
- **Varlin:** one Notre-Dame version remains unresolved. The [SIK-ISEA catalogue raisonné](https://www.sik-isea.ch/de-ch/Forschung-Publikationen/Publikationen/Publikationendetail/Varlin-e28093-Die-Bilder-Werkverzeichnis) establishes a broader research source; its publication is not a complete captured object dataset here.
- **Mario Zanini:** all 18 WikiArt entries already existed. Six unillustrated source works have unknown dates. The [MAC USP artist collection](https://acervo.mac.usp.br/acervo/index.php/Detail/entities/6188) reports 113 works, including 94 with images and 19 without; these broader holdings are not individually reconciled here. Direct metadata retrieval timed out; search-index evidence is retained separately.
- **Martin Kober:** all 17 WikiArt entries now have catalogue pictures. An exploratory KHM URL returned 404 and supplies no object evidence. Independent collection coverage remains open.
- **Douglas Huebler:** eight pre-1971 WikiArt works already existed; six later works were excluded. [MoMA’s artist collection](https://www.moma.org/artists/2749-douglas-huebler) is an additional source; the ordinary fetch returned 403, preserved without bypassing access controls.
- **Ferris:** three source listings remain held, including two differently bordered reproductions of Aristotle tutoring Alexander. Depicted events such as 1620 or 1776 were not treated as creation dates. The [Philadelphia Museum collection record](https://www.philamuseum.org/objects/69114) and archival cycle references do not establish full object coverage.
- **Hébert:** ten WikiArt listings remain identity holds. The [Musée Hébert collections](https://musees.isere.fr/page/musee-hebert-les-collections-permanentes) require further object-level reconciliation. The existing catalogue contains works outside the current WikiArt index.
- **Abdullah Suriosubroto:** 18 distinct source landscapes were added in review, with unknown creation dates and source picture links. Their images remain unattached pending date review. The [Commons painting category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Paintings_by_Abdullah_Suriosubroto) is an additional comparison source.
- **Kaoru Kawano:** all 47 WikiArt records now have catalogue pictures. [Ohmi Gallery’s print database](https://ohmigallery.com/ImageDatabase/Kawano/KawanoDB.htm) identifies additional designs and warns that its coverage and dates are incomplete; lifetime and posthumous impressions still need edition-level reconciliation.
- **Joan Brull:** 35 source listings remain identity holds. The [catalogue website](https://joanbrull.com/en/) returned 403; no access-control workaround was attempted.

There are 311 unknown-date image holds within the WikiArt delivery plans; these are not a count of every unillustrated catalogue work. Five new native museum works also have source pictures but no application attachment because their dates are unknown or fall outside the existing pre-1956 Greek museum image workflow. The 1970 National Gallery Hydra is retained as review metadata with its source image link.

## Identity and rights decisions

Exact creator identity, titles, translations, dates, dimensions, inventory numbers and visual composition were considered together. Five additional Vyzantios WikiArt records were distinguished from generic-title neighbours. Eighteen Kittelsen version decisions were resolved through contact sheets; duplicate or ambiguous representations remain held. Existing-image comparisons caught the Kittelsen white-horse water-spirit duplicate and Hébert’s harp-playing Muse under alternate titles. The source decisions, image hashes, contact indexes and preserved earlier plans document those choices.

WikiArt rights labels and credits remain separate from user source approval. Its database status remains restricted, with the approved Greek museum workflow recorded separately. The image depicts the exterior domed bathhouse with its complete frame; a correction annotation preserves an earlier review-note wording error.

All 242 application derivatives preserve the supplied frame and are at most 100,000 bytes. Uploads were create-only and public bytes were checked by SHA-256. The 317 research-only derivatives were relocated outside the deployable public folder; their original evidence manifests and relocation map remain intact. Original files and research contact sheets are archived outside Documents.
