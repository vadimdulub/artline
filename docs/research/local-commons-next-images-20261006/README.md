Reviewed 100 additional existing missing-image artworks, excluding the preceding 589 reviewed IDs. The remaining 99 records stay unattached.

The exact Commons file identifies the artwork in both wikitext and structured data and carries a public-domain label. Its originating source is RKD image record `242017`. The current RKD image-request and copyright policies were captured: they place responsibility for protected material on the user and request the full RKD credit; they do not themselves supply an open licence. The accepted basis is the exact Commons PD-Art/PDM reproduction of the historical two-dimensional painting, with no contrary photograph-specific claim established. The database preserves this distinction, both policy captures and “RKD – Netherlands Institute for Art History, The Hague” in its credit and attribution.

**This is a source-provided monochrome archival reproduction.** The full composition was visually checked: Tobit and Anna, the goat at left, the dog at their feet and the surrounding courtyard. Its original painting colours are not represented. No colour reconstruction, crop or generated image was used. This limitation is retained in database attribution and image evidence. The JPEG is 83,192 bytes.
