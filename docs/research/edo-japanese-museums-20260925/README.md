# Edo-period Japanese museum scan — 25 September 2026

35 selected official collection, exhibition-history and permissions pages captured from four Japanese museums. This is a research handoff, not a new import or image download. The separate 36-work Cleveland batch is already published locally and in production.

**Findings:** 17 dated Edo artworks (12 prints and 5 paintings), 9 book/edition records, and 10 historical-event leads. Three additional Ōta highlights are dated after Edo and are excluded from the Edo shortlist. All candidates retain source links and captured evidence.

## Holds and next import requirements

- Ōta: Kiyochika’s Ryōgoku-bashi Bridge (c.1877–82), Yoshitoshi’s Hakkenden scene (1885), and Chikanobu’s European Philharmonic Orchestra (1889) are Meiji works. They may fit the wider pre-1970 catalogue, but not an Edo-only batch.
- Printing Museum: Tsurezuregusa has a 1608 heading but “around 1600” in its description. Hokusai Manga spans 1814–1878 and needs volume/edition-level separation.
- Creator roles must remain distinct: author, publisher, translator and illustrator. The Tales of Ise illustrator is attributed, not confirmed.
- The Sumida exhibition includes works owned by other museums and private collections. Its location does not establish ownership of every listed object.
- No reuse permission was established for the shortlisted reproductions. Printing Museum requires permission for reuse outside its stated exceptions; Ōta collection pages carry copyright notices. Keep image rights unresolved and obtain a documented licence before downloading or publishing reproductions. Public-domain age of the underlying artwork is not itself a museum-image licence.
- No current on-view assertions were imported. A selected collection page or past exhibition is insufficient evidence of present display.
- The local audit was read-only. No test databases, fixtures, or new Japanese-museum records were inserted.
