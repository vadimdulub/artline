All thirteen records already existed, lacked primary images and had eligible creation dates and documented museum selection evidence. The operation preserves artwork dates, unknown accession/material/dimension fields, creator authorities, holdings and review state. No catalogue records or fixtures were created, no publication state changed and no production writes occurred. The operation name uses the Cyprus date; captures and receipts use UTC on 6 October.

Thirty-seven unique WikiArt pages supplied bounded version alternatives. Previously archived originals were reused only when the current source URL, checksum and byte count agreed.

The four unique artworks have distinctive matching composition and linework. [Ruins in Park](https://www.wikiart.org/en/karl-bryullov/ruins-in-park) matches the broken arch, relief panels, urn, steps, ivy and reflected pool in [Ж-3366](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/painting/17_19/zh-3366/index.php). [Pheb in His Chariot](https://www.wikiart.org/en/karl-bryullov/pheb-in-his-chariot) matches the figure, red drapery, ornate chariot, horses and small sails in [Ж-6547](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/painting/17_19/zh-6547/index.php). Its complete circular composition is retained. The [summer landscape drawing](https://www.wikiart.org/en/boris-kustodiev/summer-landscape-with-women) matches the two women, cloud outlines, fields and sketch lines in [Р-57907](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/drawings/r-57907/index.php). [Sunset](https://www.wikiart.org/en/boris-kustodiev/sunset) matches the backlit cloud, rays, village roofs, winding path and tree in [Ж-11160](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/painting/19_20/zh-11160/index.php). The alternative seashore Sunset differs. Strong source colour and contrast differences remain recorded and unchanged.
