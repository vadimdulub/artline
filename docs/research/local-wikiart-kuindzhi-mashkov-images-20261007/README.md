All seventeen selected source originals and sixteen native museum photographs were inspected. Kuindzhi’s Sea, Winter Sunset, Sunset Effect, Forest Lake, Volga and Cloud above a Mountain Valley match the exact native painted forms. Source and native reproductions differ noticeably in colour; the supplied source colours are retained without reconstruction. Several WikiArt dates, often marked circa, differ from the native/catalogue years or decades. Those assertions remain separate in evidence and attribution; the catalogue was not redated.

Two same-title Kuindzhi sources are different works. **Snowy Peaks, Ж-1113**, has a soft green valley and rounded slopes, unlike the source’s steep cliffs, broad white peaks and red foreground rock. **Mountain Slope, Ж-1303**, is a vertical purple slope above bright plants, unlike the horizontal rocky ledge supplied by WikiArt. Both native records remain image gaps with private comparisons and no prepared application files.

All eight Mashkov records have exact matches. The 1925 **Bakhchisaray, Ж-8142**, matches the valley, red-roofed house, poplar and foreground stones; the circa-1920 town/minaret alternative is different. **Lady with a Contrabass** matches the sitter, instrument, piano, chair, wall picture and gourds. The bread, grape, begonia and oval pineapple still lifes match their individual fruit, loaf and decorative forms. The boy portrait and lady with pheasants match faces, hands, clothing and background details.

For **Pineapple Still Life, Ж-6618**, the existing catalogue says 1908, the current native page says around 1910 and WikiArt says 1910. All three assertions remain recorded. The source’s 121 by 171 cm and native 128.6 by 172.5 cm descriptions also remain separate. The source shows the full oval image on pale corners without the museum photograph’s decorative frame. **Begonia Still Life** retains source 1910 and native/catalogue 1911. Other source dimension rounding and shorter material descriptions are preserved literally.

Three exact native-title differences were individually pinned: Хлебы versus Хлебы. Натюрморт; plural бегониями versus singular бегонией; and Дама с фазанами versus Портрет дамы с фазанами. [Native-title proofs](native-title-review.json) bind each difference to its exact URL, captured page, inventory and period. No general title relaxation or catalogue rewrite was introduced. WikiArt’s Private Collection label for the pheasant portrait conflicts with the exact museum record; it remains source evidence without creating a holding or display claim.

Five source frames are explicitly qualified: the winter-sunset study’s lower autograph; the bread and grape paintings’ upper autographs/margins; the boy portrait’s upper autograph; and the pheasant portrait’s lower shoes/dress edge and autograph. Some signature marks remain visible at the frame boundaries. [View proofs](view-scope-review.json), stored view labels, accessible text and attribution identify what the supplied frames omit. No additional cropping or recolouring was applied.

[Creator evidence](creator-identity-review.json) preserves Kuindzhi’s native birth-year uncertainty and Mashkov’s null local birth/death fields. Mashkov’s native biography is source evidence, not a database biography update. Both current linked museum author pages and Wikidata-to-WikiArt creator crosswalks were verified.
