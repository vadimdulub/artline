# Further Commons missing-image review — 6 October 2026

Reviewed **80 new existing artworks by 80 artists**, excluding previously assessed records. One image was attached: Gerrit Dou's *A Flute Player*, 1640–1645, inventory 624 at the Tiroler Landesmuseum Ferdinandeum. Its exact Wikidata artwork identity, creator, collection, inventory and Commons reproduction agree. The file explicitly carries PD-Art/Public Domain Mark; its RKD source is retained with the requested full credit, **RKD – Netherlands Institute for Art History, The Hague**.

The visually inspected reproduction shows the complete oval composition and original white outer margins. It is an archival monochrome photograph: the original painting colours are not represented. This limitation is explicit in the stored attribution and evidence. The final JPEG is 79,207 bytes, uses a proportional full-frame conversion, and has no generated content or crop.

The other 79 records remain unchanged and unattached. Seventy-six require additional identity, source or permission evidence. Three exact Commons matches remain under source-policy holds: two Salzburg Museum reproductions and Jan Jacobsz. van der Stoffe's *Huntsmen in a Landscape*, supplied through Art UK from the Ashmolean. The [Ashmolean's current ordering policy](https://www.ashmolean.org/ordering-images) reserves its photographs and requires clearance; the exact source and policy capture were retained before downloading any image bytes for that case. Existing Salzburg holds were also applied before download.

The RKD's current [image-request policy](https://www.rkd.nl/en/collection/visual-documentation/image-request) and [copyright guidance](https://www.rkd.nl/en/copyright) are preserved under `metadata/source-policies/`. They require rights-holder permission for copyright-protected works and the specified source credit. They were not treated as a blanket open licence: the image-use basis remains the exact Commons PD-Art/PDM declaration for this seventeenth-century two-dimensional work, with no contrary photograph-specific claim in that file's evidence.
