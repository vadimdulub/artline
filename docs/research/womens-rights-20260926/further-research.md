# Additional works located

These 26 additional collection/archival leads are separate from the 50 selected
artwork records. They were located through institutional collection pages and
search results; their complete metadata, creator roles, eligibility and image
permissions still need reconciliation. They are **not imported or downloaded**.
Dates omitted here remain unresolved in this research manifest. A source URL is
not a claim that an object is currently on display.
