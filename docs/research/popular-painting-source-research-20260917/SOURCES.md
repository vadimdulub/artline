Source research checked 17 September 2026 for the existing popular-painter painting gaps. Counts below describe the starting catalogue, not a promise that each museum has a downloadable image. Selection first: 1,979 missing-image paintings already have supported holdings and eligible dates; another 970 popular-painter painting gaps need other eligibility or record work. No private reference dataset was used.

The most productive new route in this pass was Städel's current object-specific download statement. Nationalmuseum's own outward links to its Wikimedia donations also recovered images after resolving title-language and legacy formatting issues. Public museum APIs remain useful, but an open metadata record or a working IIIF URL is not an image licence.

Practical next sequence: use exact museum donation links and current per-image release statements first; then alternate institutional records and independently licensed photographs. For institutions with no missing eligible record in this snapshot, curate new museum-supported paintings before downloading. For gaps with unknown dates, uncertain holdings, attribution conflicts or image-component records, resolve the catalogue question separately. Do not weaken matching rules to turn source-image availability into a higher count.

All gap counts are from `eligible-popular-painting-gaps.json` and `gap-institutions.json`. API probes, object captures, image receipts and hold reasons are retained alongside this report. Policy research is a source-prioritization result, not a claim that all these collections were fully scanned or imported.
