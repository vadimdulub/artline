# Twenty batches of ten random artists

Completed and production-verified: **200 distinct artists in 20 batches**, **2,797 new review records** and **2,818 added pictures**.

[Open the source-linked picture report](report.html). Each batch contains ten artist results and a searchable, paginated artwork table.

Complete accessible WikiArt-index research pass for 200 distinct randomly sampled artists in 20 batches. An unavailable index and unlinked listings remain explicit. This does not establish a complete lifetime oeuvre for any artist.

The run records 12,021 source listings and 11,986 source picture links. Source entries are not necessarily distinct physical objects.

## Scope and remaining gaps

- The reproducible uniform sample draws 200 artists without replacement from 2,396 unique active production person/WikiArt matches. It excludes the previous eleven-artist group; it does not favour artists with large indexes or dates before 1971. The seed, frame and all twenty groups are preserved.
- WikiArt is the approved image reference. Its actual rights labels, source pages, retrieval receipts and file hashes are retained separately from user approval. Source dates are evidence; existing catalogue dates and images were not silently replaced.
- Angela Bulloch’s text index returned an error. The captured artist API lists ten works, all dated after 1970; no eligible addition was inferred. Larry Poons has 28 index entries without artwork-page links; available index/API identity evidence and unresolved matches are retained explicitly.
- Known creation dates after 1970 are excluded. Unknown or conflicting dates remain unknown in review, with picture links preserved when delivery was held. Later editions, uncertain creator labels, detail views and duplicate candidates are not collapsed into different objects.
- Existing primary images, holdings, current-display claims and publication state are preserved. No new museum holding or display claim is asserted by this artist pass. New personal selections remain distinct from museum designations.

## Verification and evidence

- `production-preflight.json`, the 200 `applied/` and `verified/` receipts, and `summary.json` record the production outcomes. Files were uploaded with create-only object generations and verified through public URLs; database/media rights and bounded public artist APIs were checked.
- Every selected image received decoding, proportion, size, checksum and duplicate checks. Manual visual QA covers the recorded first/middle/last samples and every flagged low-resolution, uncertain match or placeholder exception; this is not a claim of manual inspection of every image. Editorial anomalies and additional architecture views have separate decisions.
- The operation used a successful Cloud SQL backup and immutable selection/delivery preimages. Original images, contact sheets, backups and held derivatives live outside Documents under Artline’s Application Support folders. `research-image-relocations.json` records held derivatives removed from the deployable assets folder.
- Final reconciliation found one Arthur Dove object added by a concurrent Met import. Visual comparison confirmed the same recto. The new duplicate was archived, its approved WikiArt image attached to the existing museum-backed object, and source identity/personal selection reconciled. Original records and evidence are retained: 2,798 gross creates, one archived duplicate, 2,797 net new active records, 2,818 pictures. See `post-delivery-reconciliations.json` and `final-completion-verification.json`.
- Current artist catalogue totals include concurrent additions outside this operation; they are not its addition counts. See `concurrent-catalogue-changes.json`. The original 200 delivery receipts remain immutable and the later correction is separately audited.
- The real local catalogue was not connected to or changed. No commits or application deployment were made, and no records were published.
