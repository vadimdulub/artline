The 1889 outdoor reading portrait is a different work.

The [Polovtsian Camp source](https://www.wikiart.org/en/nicholas-roerich/polovtsian-camp-1914-1) matches [Ж-1983](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/painting/19_20/zh-1983/index.php): two flags, the left tent’s zigzags and rounded motifs, central overlapping tents, three smoke columns, low hill contours and right-edge tent agree. The 1908 and other 1914 candidates differ in flags, tents, hills and river. This reproduction has pronounced yellow/red colour differences; no colour correction was applied. WikiArt’s 1914 date remains evidence alongside unchanged catalogue and native 1909.
