# WikiArt links in earlier artwork authorities — 6 October 2026

A metadata-only examination of 715 previously held artworks with captured authority records found three explicit Wikidata P6002 artwork links to WikiArt. The [discovery evidence](discovery.json) retains the exact earlier captures, hashes and current local state.

The leads are Jacob Jordaens’s *The Bean King*, Joan Miró’s *Portrait of Vincent Nubiola* and Lyonel Feininger’s *The Cathedral in Halle*. All three remained missing-image review records with eligible catalogue dates at this audit. The source slugs are evidence-backed leads, not approved image matches.

This audit downloaded no images and made no database changes. It adds no individually reviewed records or attachments to the combined recovery counts. Further work requires fresh authority and artwork-page captures, current local snapshots, same-work/version and creator checks, source-date review, and visual inspection under the separately documented WikiArt source approval.

A subsequent [read-only direct-ID scope count](remaining-direct-id-scope.json) found only the two previously held artworks still missing images among records already carrying WikiArt artwork IDs. Both remain eligible review records with selection evidence; individual object/component issues, rather than source-approval policy, prevent their attachment.

All three leads were subsequently resolved through fresh source and visual comparison in the [local crosswalk attachment operation](../local-wikiart-crosswalk-images-20261006/README.md). Three images were attached with original catalogue values preserved. The discovery snapshot remains historical evidence; these are revisits, not additional distinct artwork-review counts.
