# Yov Kondzelevych — selected icon research

The creator had two active artworks at selection. Eleven distinct icons were
selected for new review records, with four explicit `attributed_to` links. The
whole Maniava iconostasis and its previously catalogued Savior Not Made by Hands
were excluded. Two Pentecost source entries depict one object; only one was used.

Evidence includes the National Museum in Lviv object catalogue, the Lviv branch
of the National Research Restoration Centre's *Revived Masterpieces*, Maria
Helytovych's study in *Volyn Icon* 25 (2018), L. Obukhovych in volume 24 (2017), and
the official Volyn museum collection catalogue (2018). Volart is identified as a
supplemental regional research source, not as the holding museum. Relevant PDF
pages and all eleven delivered photographs were rendered/visually inspected.

Important decisions:

- The signed Christ the Teacher panel is dated 1698 and Ascension 1705. The
  signature transcription resolves the former's 1697 prose discrepancy.
- Christ Pantocrator is Volyn inventory І-109. The mounted Saint George is
  РА-1064 from Bobly, not the standing Okorsk icon І-228. The initially proposed
  Okorsk comparison is preserved as rejected evidence.
- The Voshchatyn apostle pairs retain qualified attribution because the documented
  ensemble involved assistants. The disputed single Bilostok apostles are a
  different group and were not substituted for these paired panels.
- The Bilostok Virgin and Pentecost retain reported attributions and unknown
  numeric dates. Conflicting late-seventeenth/early-eighteenth-century labels are
  explicit. Seven new works have no invented numeric creation year.
- The Trinity's exact ensemble placement remains unresolved. A visual resemblance
  to the 1696 altar is not used to assign that year. The Baptism's unsupported
  `fresco` medium is omitted; its explicit WikiArt 1696 date remains source-reported.
- No new holding links were made: Lviv National Art Gallery is not the National
  Museum in Lviv. Documented inventories and holdings remain in citations pending
  exact institution reconciliation. No current-display claim is made.

The approved WikiArt images preserve actual Public domain labels and separate
user source approval. Full supplied frames, damage, conservation patches and
older restoration states remain visible. No generated, reconstructed or
upscaled substitutes were used. `reviewed-plan.json.gz`, `native-research-applied.json`,
`visual-review.json`, and the verification receipts provide the precise audit.
Source PDFs/original images and recovery snapshots are under the established
Artline Library locations; the real local database was never connected.
