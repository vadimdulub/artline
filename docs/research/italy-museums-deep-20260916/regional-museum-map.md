# Italy: national source map

Source discovery covers all 20 Italian regions. Depth differs: some routes yielded individual objects; others yielded a collection index, museum homepage, or access failure. This is not a complete national collection census. A failed URL does not establish that a museum has no catalogue. England appears only in the artwork review as a holder of works by Italy-associated artists; Vatican City is a separate geography.

Local counts are active linked records of all media and dates at the audit snapshot, not museum collection totals. “Unmapped in this route” is a research gap, not proof that no equivalent local institution exists. All location mappings are suggestions; none has been written to the database.
