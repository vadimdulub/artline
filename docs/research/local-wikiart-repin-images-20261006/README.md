The [1870 Barge Haulers study](https://www.wikiart.org/en/ilya-repin/barge-haulers-on-the-volga-1-1870) matches [Ж-7928](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/painting/19_20/zh-7928/index.php), including the front-facing haulers, large sail behind them, right hillside, foreground branches and dated inscription. Its source Russian title ends in `1`. The other 1870 study and the famous finished painting are different versions.

The [1895 Satan sketch](https://www.wikiart.org/en/ilya-repin/follow-me-satan-1895) matches [Ж-2779](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/painting/19_20/zh-2779/index.php): the adjacent pale-robed and dark winged figures, flames, brushwork and rocky ledge agree. Source title `Иди за мной, Сатано` and catalogue title `«Иди за мною, Сатано»` are retained literally. The catalogue keeps its `1895 (?)` uncertainty. The lower-right inscription is partly clipped by the supplied source frame; the database view label, accessible text and attribution state that limitation.

The [first 1879 Arrest drawing](https://www.wikiart.org/en/ilya-repin/putting-a-propagandist-under-arrest-1-1879) matches [Р-7869](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/drawings/r-7869/index.php), including the headscarf, central child and man, seated figure, beams, window lines and lower-left signature. It is distinct from the other drawing and both oil paintings. The source title's `1` suffix and its monochrome reproduction of the warm-toned physical sheet remain explicit evidence.

The [second 1870 Burlak study](https://www.wikiart.org/en/ilya-repin/burlak-2-1870) matches [Ж-4055](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/painting/19_20/zh-4055/index.php), including the cap, harness, shirt, rope belt, hands, fence and dated inscription. Its source title ends in `2`. The source trims the top of the cap and narrow painting edges; database view and accessible text qualify the framing. The other two candidates depict different men/poses.

The undated [Picnic source](https://www.wikiart.org/en/ilya-repin/picnic) is a different painting: tables, chairs and a guitar player replace the native scene's ground cloth and figures playing with raised sticks. It was rejected for physical identity, not missing source approval or date. Its actual structured year `?` and absent displayed date are preserved. No source-date exception was introduced.
