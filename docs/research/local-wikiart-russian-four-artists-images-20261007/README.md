**Московский дворик — Ж-4210 (unresolved).** Native Ж-4210 is the 1902 canvas repetition with a dated lower-right autograph. The 1877 cardboard source repeats the general courtyard but has different cloud contours, paths, fence/foreground positions, church details and no matching 1902 signature. It is a different physical version, not a colour variant of this inventory.

**Константинополь — Ж-12119 (unresolved).** Native Ж-12119 (1882) has a waterside house at left, glowing orange window, steps and figures, vine-covered terrace, a small foreground boat and a ship at right beneath the moon. All six reviewed Constantinople/Bosphorus sources have different buildings, ships, shorelines and figures: the 1856 broad harbour, 1880 poplars, 1862 fortress, 1884 right mosque/ships, 1894 left mosque, and 1886 illuminated mosque. None is this canvas.

**После бури — Р-78 (unresolved).** Native Р-78 is a horizontal 1894 sepia drawing with survivors on a log raft at centre and a listing ship at left. The vertical 1854 source has an upright ship and a wreck at right; the undated colour seascape has no corresponding central raft; the added 1894 Moonrise painting shows dark waves and moonlight without this raft or drawing marks. Different artworks; no date inference or attachment.

**Черное море — Р-13074 (unresolved).** Native Р-13074 is the small 1845 watercolour with a tan lateen sail left of centre, distant ships at left and right, and its autograph at lower left. The 1881 source is the famous broad open stormy sea, without the prominent sailboat or the watercolour marks. Different physical artwork despite the shared title.

**Вид Константинополя — Ж-1787 (attached).** Exact 1846 canvas Ж-1787: mosque and paired minarets at right, orange moon just left of it, individual skyline, three-masted ship far left, narrow foreground boat with seated figures, gold reflection and fire at lower right agree. WikiArt gives 124 × 192 cm; the native inventory gives 124 × 192.5 cm. Preserve the source’s hazier reddish colour. Its English page lacks an Original Title field; the pinned Russian index explicitly names Вид Константинополя в лунном свете. This individually reviewed expanded title identifies the same native Вид Константинополя; it is not a general missing-title exception. The other three sources show different views.

**Казаки — Р-13391 (attached).** Exact watercolour study Р-13391 (1891): central pale face with red cap, large red back at right, left horizontal shooter, background weapons, dark seated foreground figure and individual line/wash marks agree, including the lower autograph and 1891. The source is darker and has a slightly tighter lower paper edge; retain the complete supplied frame. The other 1891 source is a different sheet of separate heads and figures.

**Лунная ночь — Ж-12118 (unresolved).** Native Ж-12118 (1860) has a broad dark tree canopy on the left cliff, moon beside clouds above centre-right, seated/walking figures and a bucket at lower right, and a ship at right. None of ten inspected nocturnes has these exact trees, people and shoreline: the first seven show different fortresses, beaches or ships, while the added Capri 1841, Black Sea 1859 and Crimea 1862 views show different boats, cliffs and a lone tall cypress. No matching canvas identified.

**Енисей — Р-13389 (unresolved).** Native Р-13389 is a vertical 1891 watercolour with dark blue ridges, green island at centre-right, log rafts across the foreground and lower handwriting. The undated source is a broad horizontal river vista with different distant slopes and no foreground raft. A different composition; no unknown-date exception is appropriate.

**Боярышня — Р-50225 (unresolved).** Native Р-50225 is the loose 1899 graphite drawing of a woman turned left, ornate headgear, large outlined arms and a prominent dedication/date below. The 1886 source is a finished colour oil portrait of a front-facing woman wrapped in patterned robes and a pale scarf. Different figure, work and technique.

**За кого Меня почитают люди? — Р-6410 (unresolved).** Native Р-6410 is the 1900s watercolour repetition: blue garment on the left disciple, Christ bareheaded with different face/hand placement, angular washes and a smaller bridge opening. The source image is the finished oil composition with different heads, dark robes, masonry and water; its second page is a vertical detail of that oil. Matching listed dimensions do not override the visibly different version and medium.

**Портрет А. И. Казначеева — Ж-1778 (attached).** Exact 1848 canvas Ж-1778: black-cloaked sitter, diagonal bright sash, star medal, gloved hand holding a hat, individual cloak folds and lower-left dated inscription agree. Preserve the source’s stronger red/green colour balance. The expanded source title identifies Senator Alexander Ivanovich Kaznacheyev, corresponding to the native initials А. И. Казначеев. The alternative 1847 source is a different oval bust with red background.

**Солдат с ружьем — Ж-4234 (attached).** Exact 1898 oil study Ж-4234: crouching soldier with raised left knee, tricorn edging, windblown dark coat at left, red collar, both hands on the diagonal long gun, individual brushwork and lower-left autograph agree. Preserve the darker source reproduction and complete supplied frame. The other source shows an upright front-facing soldier with a different pose and gun.

**Демон поверженный — Р-2437 (unresolved).** Native Р-2437 is the 1901 watercolour/graphite/ink study with dense angular hatching, a large pale foreground arm and layered wing segments to the right. The 1902 source is the finished tempera painting; the additional 1901 source is a different quick linear reclining-figure sketch with a long bare body and largely blank paper. Neither reproduces this specific study.

**Казак с ружьем — Р-13386 (unresolved).** Native Р-13386 is the 1901 ink/graphite drawing with broad black sleeve, curled moustache, saber at belt, complete long gun and lower-right autograph. The 1893 watercolour has a different fur cap, pale coat, hand and body positions, gun and lower-left signature. Same general subject, different physical drawing.

**Морской пролив с маяком — Ж-7914 (attached).** Exact canvas Ж-7914: short lighthouse left of centre, individual left cliff profile and moored ship, middle rowing boat, two-sailed boat at right, small background sails, mountain silhouette, cloud contours and lower-left autograph agree. WikiArt dates it 1873, while the native inventory/catalogue dates it 1841. This source discrepancy is recorded without changing either claim or the catalogue. Retain the warmer yellow-green source colour and full supplied frame.
