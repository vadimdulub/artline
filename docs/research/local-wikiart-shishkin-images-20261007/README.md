**Pine Forest, Р-13261**, matches the 1889 graphite drawing's broad pale right trunk, arching branches, dark left tree, foliage clusters, foreground hatching and lower-right inscription. The high-contrast grayscale reproduction is retained separately from the native warm paper tone. Eight same-title colour paintings show different trunks, paths and clearings.

The eight unresolved records are **Self-Portrait, Р-11060; Forest, Гр.-18958; Oaks, Р-39496; Oak, Гр.-18936; In the Wild North, Р-11055; and Gurzuf, Гр.-18762, Гр.-18767 and Гр.-18768**.
