**Five verified WikiArt reproductions are saved locally and attached to existing artwork records.** Four Kustodiev drawings and one Serov drawing match their exact native photographs. Bryullov’s unfinished portrait remains unmatched after all six dated indexed self-portraits were reviewed. Catalogue dates, titles, unknown fields, creator links, holdings and review state are unchanged. Captures and receipts use UTC on 6 October; the operation name uses the local Cyprus date.

All six selected records already existed, lacked primary images, had documented museum selection evidence and had eligible creation dates. Native object pages and linked artist biographies corroborate the existing UUIDs, object identifiers, accessions and creator life dates; captured Wikidata records connect those creators to WikiArt. Unknown local accession, medium and dimension fields remain unknown.

Twenty-one unique WikiArt pages support 24 object/version comparisons, with at most six alternatives per record. Sixteen source images, all six native photographs and all five final JPEGs were visually inspected. Five candidates were excluded on explicit object, material or holding evidence before image download: two Kustodiev paintings at other museums, the previously matched Bryullov oil painting, and two Serov works of different material and collection. Initial eighteen-page metadata and the earlier candidate/proof files remain in `history/`.

The [large-bow-tie drawing Р-53189](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/drawings/r-53189/index.php) matches the turned head, moustache and beard, outlined bow, rust-coloured shoulder and violet wash. The [frontal drawing Р-49273](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/drawings/r-49273/index.php) instead has a small dark red bow, white collar highlights, hatched jacket and the Paris inscription. They are distinct 1904 sheets and receive different source images. Small lower-right collection marks visible in the native photographs are not visible in these reproductions; that limitation is preserved in their individual notes.

The [seated drawing Р-8127](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/drawings/r-8127/index.php) matches the right-facing profile, folded hands, jacket, chair, lower-left inscription and lower-right paper damage. Its current source Date explicitly states 1910–1914, matching the catalogue and native date interval. The [1918 drawing РС-801](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/drawings/rs-801/index.php) matches the frontal head, broad moustache, patterned bow, coat button, angular tinted background, signature/date and collection stamp. Source colour and tonal differences remain unaltered.

All four selected Kustodiev pages have the literal Original Title `Автопортрет. 1902.jpg`. Four individually scoped concordances preserve this filename-like field without interpreting the suffix as the creation year. Explicit Date fields, native identities, inscriptions where present and exact visual comparisons establish the selected works. No global title normalization or year rewrite was introduced.

The [Serov drawing Р-13405](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/drawings/r-13405/index.php) matches the loose hair strands, moustache, cheek shading, dense background hatching, white collar and diagonal shoulder strokes. Source 1887 remains evidence alongside unchanged native/catalogue 1883. Its closer framing omits broad sheet margins and the lower-right collection mark. The stored association, accessible description and attribution explicitly identify that view; the complete supplied source image is retained without further crop.

The [unfinished Bryullov portrait Р-2225](https://rusmuseumvrm.ru/data/collections/drawings/r-2225/index.php) has a small softly coloured head near the upper centre of a largely blank sheet, with faint unfinished body lines. The source dated 1830–1833 instead shows a large ink profile with a completed coat and neckwear. The 1813–1816, 1823, 1836 and 1848 sources also show different compositions. The 1833 oil portrait is already matched to the separate native painting Ж-5077. No application derivative was prepared for this record.

All five selected sources actually assert public domain, recorded separately from [user approval](source-authorization.json) and the [image policy](../../ARTLINE_IMAGE_USE.md#user-approved-wikiart-source-policy--6-october-2026). Native photographs and unused candidates remain private. Selected originals were reused from their checksum-verified private comparison captures. Derivatives preserve the complete supplied frames, using proportional resizing and JPEG compression only.

Sixty-one identity/version/title/date/view checks passed: five positive and 56 rejection controls. These include wrong creator, object, accession and native title; unapproved source-title variants; removal of the four literal concordances; omission of Serov’s date difference and view qualification; and rejection of the unfinished Bryullov mismatch. All six live records and creator authorities were unchanged immediately before attachment. Locked preimages preceded five database attachments. Final read-only checks verified files, originals, complete rights/provenance evidence, identities, holdings and unchanged catalogue fields. The combined recovery verifies 939 attachments across 1,834 distinct reviewed artworks, including 22 explicitly qualified views. HTTP delivery was not reverified.
