# More Russian Museum title variants — 7 October 2026

**Eighteen verified WikiArt images were saved locally and attached to existing review artworks.** Twenty-three records were selected from the [title-variant discovery](../local-wikiart-russian-title-variants-20261007/README.md). Catalogue titles, dates, unknown fields, creator links, holdings and publication states were preserved.

Thirty-one distinct WikiArt pages supplied 32 initial record-level alternatives. Two clearly different works were excluded before photo downloads: the large 1900 oil portrait of Nordman and the circa-1841 oil portrait of Alexander Bryullov. Twenty-nine source reproductions and all twenty-three native museum photographs were visually compared. One already captured alternative was individually added to the second Uzbek-boy record, bringing the final record-level options to 33 without another source download. Eighteen literal WikiArt title concordances were accepted after individual object/version review.

Five records remain unchanged and unattached: Petrov-Vodkin's worker-family watercolour **РС-1431**, Repin's Nordman sanguine portrait **Р-7839**, Repin's Moscow painting **Ж-2777**, Petrov-Vodkin's seated-woman drawing **Р-1788**, and his Holy Fool costume sheet **РС-24301**. Each differs visibly from all reviewed source alternatives. They have no prepared application files.

The two Uzbek-boy portraits were distinguished by their actual painted features: **ЖБ-1276** has long side braids and a tilted sideways gaze; **Ж-10968** has an unbraided head, white diagonal collar and lower-left monogram. The initial title lead for the latter pointed to the former. The correct already captured alternative was added under a [single documented source-option expansion](metadata/individual-source-option-expansion.json), with the original candidate snapshot archived and catalogue preimages preserved.

WikiArt labels Bryullov's Basin portrait as Tretyakov Gallery, 34.6 × 27.8 cm; the native Russian Museum page records **Р-2206**, 19 × 15 cm. Matching autograph, collection stamp, inventory annotation and individual wash marks establish the same photographed sheet. Both conflicting source statements remain evidence, without changing holdings or dimensions. Kustodiev's Fortune drawing retains the visible **Рс779** inventory annotation; the source calls it a tailpiece and the native page calls it a chapter-I headpiece. The Костенич/Костинич surname forms, Romanova title's literal `1908.jpg` suffix and source medium/date differences likewise remain recorded individually.

Repin's **Ж-4087** is explicitly attached as **Detail: two seated women and man pulling on a glove**. The supplied WikiArt image omits the leftmost woman and most of her male companion. Stored view label, alt text and [framing evidence](view-scope-review.json) state this limitation; it is not represented as the complete multi-subject sheet. No further cropping or recolouring was applied.

The Holy Fool native page adds the play author to the imported title. A [single native-title concordance](native-title-concordance.json), constrained by object URL, inventory, period and creator, preserves both descriptions. It does not bypass the failed visual match. Twelve literal native-photo aliases are frozen from the corresponding museum pages; no general photo-folder bypass was added.

All eighteen current WikiArt pages assert public domain, separately from [user source approval](source-authorization.json). Complete-source-frame JPEGs are at most **97,885 bytes**; larger originals and native museum comparison references remain separately archived.
