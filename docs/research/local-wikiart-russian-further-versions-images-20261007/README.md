**РС-4027 — approved.** Exact native РС-4027 underwater design: the red-sailed vessel and island silhouettes above curling white waves, hooked large pike, two sturgeon, patterned smaller fish and lower-left 1928 monogram agree in their individual outlines and positions. The initial 1902 cavalry and 1904 city-entry illustrations are different scenes. The specifically titled Underwater source resolves this 1928 design through a published colour reproduction; its book caption and white surround are retained. The native original sheet margins are not established by this published reproduction. Source colour and contrast differ and remain unaltered; no catalogue metadata is rewritten. Editorial same-work confidence exceeds 90%, based on the separately compared native and source photographs and exact object identity.

**Ж-4522 — held.** Native Ж-4522 has a larger foreground widow leaning her bent arm against the cabinet with her other arm hanging down, a green right wall and a differently placed portrait. The undated source shows a smaller figure with a hand at her chest, outstretched cabinet arm, paneled right wall and a different cradle/furniture arrangement. This is a distinct Widow version; matching subject and creator do not establish the Russian Museum canvas.

**Р-6188 — held.** Native Р-6188 is the loose 1891 full-scene study with a black shoulder cape, pale hut, foreground bear and surrounding trees. The 1897 source is the more finished full painting with a light cape and different hands, foliage and bear; the additional 1891 source isolates a standing youth beside a wall without the bear and full woodland scene. Neither is the native study, despite the matching year of the latter.

**Ж-12114 — held.** Native Ж-12114 is a broad open meadow with a standing boy at left, two seated children, dogs, sheep and distant cows. The source has three children beside a rustic fence among birches and nearby cows. These are different Shepherds compositions, independently of the source 1903–1904 and native 1887 dates.

**Ж-4343 — held.** Native Ж-4343 shows Nesterov in a dark jacket with a raised hand holding his lapel and a dark ground. The 1915 source wears a hat beside a river; the 1918 source is a close monochrome head viewed from below; the additionally found 1928 source wears a white artist coat with hands at his pockets and brushes at right. None matches the native dark-jacket painting; the shared 1928 date does not establish identity.

**Ж-1224 — held.** Native Ж-1224 is a small left-facing bust study with an inventory label at lower right. The source is a larger seated portrait facing toward the viewer, with both hands, walking stick and books at right. These are different physical versions of the same sitter, supported by the different compositions and native 28.5 by 21.5 cm versus source 95 by 69 cm dimensions.

**Р-54312 — held.** Native Р-54312 is a 1906 three-quarter graphite/sanguine portrait with an open paper lower area and diagonal hatching. The 1915 source is an outdoor oil portrait wearing a hat, and the 1918 source is a heavily shaded, upward-looking close face. Neither source shows the native 1906 sheet.

**Р-40291 — held.** Native Р-40291 is a pale 1909 pencil drawing of the artist seated with a book beside a table, jugs and window. The source is a 1905 oil portrait with a large palette and brushes in the foreground. This is a different composition and physical object.

**Ж-11052 — held.** Native Ж-11052 is a horizontal lake view with an autumn forest reflected in water and a small central waterside structure. The source is a vertical birch-framed woodland view with a moon and winding watercourse. These are different Autumn Landscape compositions, beyond the source 1934 and native circa-1923 dates.

**Р-40881 — held.** Native Р-40881 is the tall narrow mural study of an elderly white-bearded saint in an ochre mantle, decorated dark monastic headwear, red crosses, long scroll and blue staff. The 1899 source is a square landscape painting of a younger dark-haired monk; the separately labelled undated sketch is a colourful preparatory version of that landscape figure. Both sources differ from the native Abastumani mural study.

**Гр.Луб.-2863 — held.** Native Гр.Луб.-2863 is a complete chromolithograph showing a large room, many seated and standing figures, striped rugs and an elaborate surrounding caption border. The source is a smaller page design of diners above a central blank panel and two text columns. This is a different feast design, not a reproduction or crop of the native print.

**Р-6190 — held.** Native Р-6190 and the undated source share a fishing-saint subject but show distinct versions. In the native 1890 sheet the white flowering tree stands left of the hut; in the source the white flowers stand to its right near the two pale trunks. The hut, basket, foreground tufts, rod angle and forest shapes also differ. This is not merely a colour or contrast difference; do not attach the other version.

**РС-2609 — approved.** Exact native РС-2609 battle design: the banner bearing Christ at upper right, ranks of pale horses, central green-clad archer viewed from behind, right striped rider, foreground red turban and shield rim match in their fine outlines and placement. The source is a high-contrast reproduction with a black border and white lower surround; it is framed slightly tighter at the lower design edge and does not establish original sheet margins. Keep the source 1935 and native/catalogue 1932 dates separately. This is distinct from the elaborate 1941 РС-4062 composition, which lacks the Christ banner and has a large central red-green shield. Editorial same-work confidence exceeds 90%, based on the separately compared native and source photographs and exact object identity.

**РС-4062 — held.** Native РС-4062 is the elaborate 1941 battle scene with a large central red-green shield, many lower horses, long crossing upper lances and no Christ banner. The source instead matches the separate 1932 РС-2609 design with the Christ banner, central rear-facing archer and a much tighter foreground. Do not reuse that image for this different inventory.

**Ж-1848 — approved.** Exact native Ж-1848, the 248 by 248 cm 1899 painting: the central white-robed dark-haired saint with staff, small left fir, church buildings at upper left, winding path and stream at right, tree silhouettes and lower-right signature all agree. The source reproduces the same complete square composition with stronger contrast and colour; those source differences are preserved. This is distinct from the narrow Abastumani mural study and the separately inspected preliminary landscape sketch. Editorial same-work confidence exceeds 90%, based on the separately compared native and source photographs and exact object identity.

**Р-40899 — approved.** Exact native Р-40899 Abastumani mural study: Christ’s raised arm and halo, robe folds and sandals, kneeling woman, two faces beside the cave, wrapped Lazarus, pale cave pillar, long foreground slab, lower-right sapling and signature strokes agree. The source is a monochrome reproduction of the coloured native sheet, with a slightly tighter left edge, rather than evidence that the original is monochrome. Preserve this qualification in the view label, alt text and attribution. WikiArt leaves its date unknown; the exact native inventory identifies the 1898–1899 study and establishes eligibility without inventing WikiArt years or changing the catalogue range. Editorial same-work confidence exceeds 90%, based on the separately compared native and source photographs and exact object identity.
