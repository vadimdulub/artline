Twenty-three distinct source pages supplied 24 record-level alternatives. All twenty-three source reproductions and twenty-one native museum photographs were visually compared; the same completed Demidov portrait was checked against two distinct native studies. Seventeen literal source-title concordances were accepted only after individual object/version comparison. Two canonical Bryullov page links were taken from the current artist list for three selected records. Four records remain unchanged and unattached: the pilgrim painting **Ж-3355**, model drawing **Р-49538**, Demidov graphite sheet **Р-28936** and Demidov oil study **Ж-3363**. Differences in composition, pose, horse harness, figures, technique and background rule out the selected sources. These records have no prepared application files. The pilgrim source's absent English Original Title is not the reason for its hold; its photograph is of a different version.

Grabar's portrait **Ж-4359** matches exactly despite WikiArt's displayed **1915** date: the native/catalogue date and the inscription visible in both photographs read **1916**. These distinct assertions remain evidence and the catalogue date is unchanged. WikiArt's **1833–1835** range for the unfinished boat portrait is retained alongside the native/catalogue **1830s**; its conflicting source dimensions are also preserved. The actual unfinished painted figures and boat establish the match.

Repin's arrival-of-the-tsars illustration **Р-7955** is described by WikiArt as oil on canvas and by the museum as gouache on cardboard. Both statements remain recorded without a catalogue media change. WikiArt's Tretyakov holding label and dimensions for Ferzen **Р-13102**, and its different dimensions for Kuindzhi **Ж-4076**, are retained separately from the matching native inventory evidence. No holdings or current-display claims were changed.

The Zhirkevich source reverses the sitter initials to **В.А.**; its exact 1891 pencil lines, autograph and collection stamp identify native **Р-7932**. This individual literal concordance preserves the source title and does not introduce a general initials-normalization rule. Shorter source titles and the Щеголенков/Щеголенок surname variants are recorded similarly.

Three supplied reproductions are monochrome: Stepan Razin's panel design **РС-1437**, the Alarm preparatory drawing **РС-1424**, and Zhirkevich **Р-7932**. Their colour was preserved as supplied. Alarm also has the explicit view label **Preparatory drawing; lower sheet margin partly outside source frame**: part of the lower autograph/date area and sheet edge is absent from the WikiArt frame. Stored alt text and [framing evidence](view-scope-review.json) retain that limitation. Its creation is 1925; 1919 is the depicted year and 1934 identifies the later painting. No further cropping or reconstruction was applied.

All seventeen current WikiArt pages assert public domain, separately from [user source approval](source-authorization.json). Complete-source-frame application JPEGs are at most **99,062 bytes**; larger originals and native comparison references remain separately archived.
