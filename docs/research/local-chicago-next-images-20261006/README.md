# Further Chicago photographs — 6 October 2026

Fifty images are saved locally and attached to fifty of sixty further existing artworks: thirty prints and twenty drawings. The selection excludes all eighty records in the two earlier Chicago groups. Ten records remain held for creator-authority evidence: eight lack a corroborating life date, one has conflicting life dates and one has a known-year discrepancy. Their catalogue records remain unchanged.

Exact current native object IDs, inventories, titles, date wording and bounds, work types and creator identities were checked before image selection. Each photograph has an explicit CC0 Public Domain Designation on its exact native gallery image, independently corroborated object/image association, and captured museum image-policy evidence. API metadata licences are not used as photograph permission.

Seven 843-pixel preview URLs returned HTTP 403. The same pages published explicitly licensed download buttons for those same photographs. Those exact download URLs succeeded for Millet, Leandro Bassano, Cavedone, Canaletto, Callot, Reni and Géricault. The failed URLs, error events and original selected evidence are retained. Only the delivery URL changed; image identity and all source-clearance requirements remained in place. Seven positive selections and five negative delivery controls passed.

Procaccini's *The Holy Family* has a gallery CC0 label without a credit-link URL. The same native page separately labels the primary photograph CC0 and links its image-licensing policy within the artwork content. That explicit evidence resolves the page-layout variation; no blank field is interpreted as permission. Four negative controls reject missing or conflicting labels and missing policy references.

Three contact sheets and full sources for Renoir, Canaletto, Toulouse-Lautrec and Gauguin were inspected. All fifty complete native frames are retained without crop, recolouring or generated content. Faint red-chalk and graphite details, original paper margins, mounts and source credit strips remain visible. Application JPEGs are at most 99,670 bytes.

Toulouse-Lautrec's *Sketchbook, 1880* is explicitly labelled as one illustrated page showing horses and handlers in a circus ring. Canaletto's *Architectural Sketches* photograph retains its visible museum copyright-credit strip. The current native gallery separately designates this exact photograph CC0. Both facts are retained in the image evidence and attribution; the strip and faint native drawing have not been removed or enhanced.

All fifty application files, private source hashes, primary-image references, artwork/media links and complete stored rights evidence passed verification. Artwork metadata, review status, creator records/aliases/identifiers and holding assertions are unchanged.
