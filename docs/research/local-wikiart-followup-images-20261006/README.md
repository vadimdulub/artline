# Local WikiArt follow-up — 6 October 2026

All 59 selected existing artworks now have visually reviewed WikiArt pictures saved locally and attached in the local database. This follow-up spans 24 creators and excludes all sixty records from the earlier WikiArt operation. It creates no catalogue records, fixtures, holdings or publication decisions.

The [separate source-approval receipt](source-authorization.json) pins the updated project policy. Fifty-eight images retain their copyright/Fair Use assertions as `restricted`; Raden Saleh’s *Portrait of Ary Prins* retains the general public-domain label. Restricted images have no independent rights-verification stamp. Approval does not replace or rewrite the source’s actual assertions.

Selection used exact existing WikiArt object IDs, PostgreSQL pre-1971 eligibility and existing collection-selection evidence. Every current page was checked for object ID, creator URL/name, title, structured and displayed dates, image identity and all rights badges. Seven images use captured Wikidata-to-WikiArt creator crosswalks. The source’s circa-1947 wording for Sekoto’s *Prayer in Church* remains in the image evidence; the existing catalogue dates are preserved. Approximate dates at the 1970 boundary require individual evidence before selection or attachment.

All images were inspected in three contact sheets, with full-source inspection of three Botong compositions and three Behzad reproductions. Behzad’s *finished Youth*, *Oldman and harp* and *Omar Khayyam* preserve their printed Fundación Cultural Oriente / www.islamoriente.com credit strips and Editorial Soroosh references. These credits are also recorded in attribution and protected by negative controls. No images were held in this operation. Native frames, borders, captions and signatures are retained, with proportional JPEG compression and no cropping. The largest application image is 98,302 bytes.

Verification passed for all 59 local files, archived originals, primary-image references, associations, actual rights labels, complete stored source evidence and before/after catalogue snapshots. All unknown work types, artist records/aliases/identifiers, holding assertions and review statuses remain unchanged. The source validators also passed 59 positive checks and six negative controls for wrong object IDs, image URLs, rights labels and removed reproduction credits. There is no new HTTP-delivery receipt following the earlier local server timeouts. Production was not changed.
