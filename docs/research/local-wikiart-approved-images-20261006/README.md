# Local WikiArt images under explicit source approval — 6 October 2026

Fifty-eight WikiArt images are saved locally and attached to fifty-eight of sixty reviewed existing missing-image records. Twelve retain WikiArt's general public-domain label. Forty-six retain copyright, Fair Use or territorial public-domain assertions as `restricted`. The user's explicit WikiArt approval is recorded separately; restricted images are not represented as independently licensed or independently rights-verified.

This follows the user's instruction to read the newly updated Markdown policy and use WikiArt as a fully approved source. The [authorization receipt](source-authorization.json) pins copies of `AGENTS.md` and `docs/ARTLINE_IMAGE_USE.md`. A missing public-domain label does not block this workflow. No further source-policy approval was requested.

The read-only selection contains sixty existing exact WikiArt artwork IDs across 41 creators. PostgreSQL established pre-1971 catalogue eligibility and existing selection evidence before source research. Current source object IDs, titles, creator URLs and names, creation wording, image paths and all supplied rights badges were checked. Ten approved images use freshly captured Wikidata P6002 crosswalks from their existing local creator authorities. No artist identifiers or missing life dates were inserted.

WikiArt often renders a copyright-owner badge and a separate Fair Use badge on the same image. Both are retained. Territorial labels such as `Public domain China` remain `restricted`, with their exact wording preserved. A general `Public domain` badge is distinguished from those territorial assertions. Source pages, links, image variants and credit markup remain in the stored evidence. No artwork age or user approval has been converted into a public-domain claim.

Three contact sheets and five full sources were inspected. The complete supplied frames are retained without cropping, recolouring or generated reconstruction. Botong Francisco's *Filipino Struggles Through History* is explicitly labelled as a pictured mural section, including the limitation in accessible text and database view labels. Behzad's *Rudaki* keeps its visible Fundación Cultural Oriente and Editorial Soroosh credit strip, also described in attribution. Qi Baishi's wisteria image retains its original Russian caption and 1944 date. Application JPEGs are at most 98,151 bytes.

Two images remain held for object or scope evidence, not source-rights policy. The *Ilekun Aafin* page describes a 207 × 88 cm door with six specific registers, but its photograph is a Galerie Walu composite of a 200 × 79 cm door with differing carved figures. Chabet's *Untitled* is described as an installation dated circa 1970 and represented by a plant-study text sheet; the cutoff and component view need individual evidence. Both original images and prepared derivatives remain private, without database image associations or public files. Their catalogue records are unchanged.

All fifty-eight attachments retain the original catalogue metadata, unknown work types, review status, creator records/aliases/identifiers and holding assertions. No artwork, artist, fixture, holding or publication record was created. Production was not changed. Files, source hashes, primary-image references, media links and complete stored evidence were checked. No new HTTP-delivery receipt is claimed following earlier local server timeouts.
