## Put Down Your Whip

Chinese resistance: 1939 portrait of performer Wang Ying associated with the anti-invasion street play. Distinct from Situ Qiao’s 1940 painting with the same English title.

Catalogue UUID: `5cc42b23-2d2b-5ba5-8722-f5917b7723d4`. Image SHA-256: `1a3c80288b3cd52a00cd790d890907b286297d27138a8f5f4744d87893ccee34`.

Stored licence/credit label: Public domain (WikiArt per-object label)

## Battle on the Bank of the Halha

Japanese official war painting, made in 1941 about the 1939 Khalkhin Gol fighting. Read critically as an imperial representation, not a neutral eyewitness photograph.

Catalogue UUID: `642d0d14-32b1-5aac-8a40-e6e72e233d0e`. Image SHA-256: `c16f338a3473fe6a65cdd8963a52eab9a84da1aab03312ecc115ab1222d11284`.

Stored licence/credit label: Tsuguharu Foujita Fair Use (WikiArt source label)

## Suicidal Attu Island

Japanese official war painting of the 1943 Attu battle. The stored title is retained; the institutional English title is Final Fighting on Attu. No heroic endorsement is implied.

Catalogue UUID: `2ba542f8-bcc5-50c0-bfdb-7b9ed64c9b73`. Image SHA-256: `8eb1d1c4dc5e6511d5bea974bd1872a721d086a77db3efb83cfd1f0fbd205a98`.

Stored licence/credit label: Tsuguharu Foujita Fair Use (WikiArt source label)

## Yellow Houses: A Street in Sophiatown

South African civilian and urban context under racial segregation. Neither a battlefield record nor proof of the artist’s wartime political affiliation.

Catalogue UUID: `d0635f59-1bc5-5127-8506-c4ef3a5590e2`. Image SHA-256: `88ea5665d6d5125099fa318cabb245dbb257a2daa3ece9e3ba83d6db62a168a9`.

Stored licence/credit label: Gerard Sekoto Fair Use (WikiArt source label)

## Convicts Cutting a Hedge

South African prison labour and unequal civilian life during the war years. Included as social context; no assertion that WWII caused the depicted imprisonment.

Catalogue UUID: `620a5009-243b-5ef0-9795-88b7d590f460`. Image SHA-256: `f370b6817c76c4b35ad9cd986223d96c908a606f4ac11c1d1732ae738e228181`.

## The Song of the Pick

Postwar labour context, 1946–47, based on an Andrew Goldie photograph from the 1930s. Neither a 1945 painting nor a WWII battle scene.

Catalogue UUID: `b940a553-233d-5a3c-b34c-8d8d848a5f1a`. Image SHA-256: `8f7608f59d8422b4faf060dd93987ba5ecdecf1138960251eff82e795cacc652`.

Stored licence/credit label: Gerard Sekoto Fair Use (WikiArt)

## Gandhi March (Bapuji)

Anti-colonial prelude, 1930. The depicted march predates WWII; this print does not depict the 1942 Quit India movement.

Catalogue UUID: `0b95593a-88ca-59ac-9046-b4d895daa13b`. Image SHA-256: `7b5cfbdc8e731eba3052b53b1eb32e556c54b2d5a6a5eb7fce2a216c9f412ed1`.

Stored licence/credit label: Copyright protected (WikiArt label)

## Kami Present, Ibu Pertiwi (Stand Guard for our Motherland)

Indonesian postwar national memory, 1965. Retains the gallery’s already supplied cropped reproduction and explicit restricted credit. Not presented as a contemporaneous WWII image or an identified battle.

Catalogue UUID: `89627e93-f2d0-5b9f-ad01-366ef3626e5d`. Image SHA-256: `aea2e9f979eb7ab901a0d10c3447ea80940533e376787dcfb4789a19f1add5ac`.

Stored licence/credit label: National Gallery Singapore site copyright notice; no explicit open image licence supplied. Published primary source filename identifies a cropped reproduction; the full supplied JPEG is retained. Embedded rights notices: Oil on canvas.
