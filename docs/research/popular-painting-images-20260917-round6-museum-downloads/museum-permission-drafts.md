## Belvedere: clarify which exact files remain CC0

Recipient: repro@belvedere.at. Confirmed on the [official Image Archive page](https://www.belvedere.at/en/researchcenter).

The [Open Content page](https://sammlung.belvedere.at/opencontent/images) describes CC0 images, while current individual image captions, including [Sonja Knips](https://sammlung.belvedere.at/objects/3197/sonja-knips), restrict the supplied photograph to private/scientific use and request contact for commercial use. This is an exact-file licensing conflict, not an assumption about the copyright of the painted original.

Subject: Artline — CC0/CC BY collection images and clarification of Open Content downloads

Dear Belvedere Image Archive team,

I maintain Artline, a public online atlas of painters and museum-held artworks: https://artline-web-lpuqqlugnq-ew.a.run.app/. I would like to include credited images of the selected paintings listed below, with links to their Belvedere records.

Your Open Content page describes CC0 image downloads, while some individual photographs currently display a private/scientific-use notice. Could you identify the exact image files for these works that remain available under CC0, Public Domain Mark, CC BY or CC BY-SA, with the complete licence URI and required photographer credits? If an older reproduction has an existing open release, its stable download link and release evidence would also be useful.

Artline stores and serves proportionally resized JPEG images from Google Cloud Storage (up to 1,200 pixels, about 100 KB). The service is publicly accessible and may support commercial use in the future. We would preserve credits, source links, licence links and any applicable ShareAlike terms. We seek permission covering the particular digital photographs and any necessary underlying artwork rights, rather than relying on artwork age alone.

If none of these files is openly available, would you consider releasing selected reproductions under CC BY 4.0 or CC0? Please identify any third-party rights or charges before providing a file; this inquiry does not place an order.

Thank you,
Vadim Dulub
