# Round 2: Anna Ancher, Elisabeth Jerichau-Baumann and El Greco

Research checked 11 September 2026. This is a bounded second-pass cohort, not a completed catalogue-wide round.

## Findings

The cohort establishes fifteen additional reusable reproductions for existing museum-connected paintings: six Anna Ancher works and seven Elisabeth Jerichau-Baumann works at Statens Museum for Kunst (SMK), and two El Greco paintings at Cleveland. The SMK object records identify their supplied images with Public Domain Mark 1.0; the two Cleveland records identify theirs as CC0. Cleveland's policy distinguishes designated open-access images from restricted images; the policy is not blanket permission for every object. [Cleveland image policy](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access), [SMK object API documentation](https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/docs/).

Fourteen artwork reviews are complete for this bounded pass, including one previously present SMK image. Five artwork reviews remain blocked. An image can be technically and legally usable while its catalogue date needs clarification: two new reproductions belong to records whose research boxes deliberately remain unchecked.

No new masterpiece designation is justified merely by these pictures being available. They are selected study reproductions of documented museum holdings. A museum's possession, a museum-highlight designation, an owner's favourite and current gallery display are separate facts. No new current-display assertion is established here.

### Why the fisherman's wife is not a clean creation-date interval

KMS3192's structured dates run from 1874 to 1912. The accompanying note says the lower bound is based on the artist's years and the upper bound on arrival at the museum. These are not equivalent to documented commencement and completion of the painting. Treating them as an ordinary production interval would give the timeline false precision. The existing record remains unchanged and blocked pending a sourced dating decision. [Exact SMK record](https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/art/?object_number=KMS3192&lang=en).

### Existing Skagens image

The named Wikimedia Commons file identifies the painting, accession 222 and a public-domain reproduction. However, a source-page label is not proof that an older local derivative was made from that exact file. The local asset is 1,957,721 bytes and has no stored image-rights-evidence row. Its source-to-file provenance and a compliant smaller derivative remain follow-up work. The existing file was preserved, not counted as newly verified. [Exact Commons file and licensing statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anna_Ancher_-_Sunlight_in_the_blue_room_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg).

The six completed reviews do not finish Ancher's painter review: two current works are blocked, and broader Skagens discovery is still open.

### Queen Olga: a Greek subject is not an artist attribution

The KMS8604 creator field assigns Jerichau-Baumann, but the inscription field records “Prossalendi” and 1868. That is a material discrepancy requiring reconciliation. A signature transcription, copied inscription, historical attribution and modern catalogue attribution need not mean the same thing. No replacement artist is established by this evidence alone. The work was neither reassigned to a Greek painter nor supplied with a new image. [Exact SMK record](https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/art/?object_number=KMS8604&lang=en).

### Date precision and sitter qualifications

For KMS8605, the approximate-1860 note and 1858–1861 bounds are compatible. The title's probable identification of the sitter remains qualified. By contrast, KMS9034 and KMS9035 have parenthesized dates in source notes but unqualified year values in the existing catalogue. Without a documented interpretation of SMK's notation, those reviews remain open. No arbitrary conversion to an exact or approximate year was made. [KMS8605 source](https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/art/?object_number=KMS8605&lang=en), [KMS9034 source](https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/art/?object_number=KMS9034&lang=en), [KMS9035 source](https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/art/?object_number=KMS9035&lang=en).

## Reproduction quality and limitations

New images range from 34,736 to 99,681 bytes, totalling 1,215,401 bytes. They retain the museum-supplied digital frame with aspect-preserving resize and JPEG compression. No AI reconstruction, recolouring or additional crop was applied. Some SMK source filenames and flags indicate museum-side cropping or reconstruction; retaining that supplied image does not establish that the reproduction shows the complete physical object or unprocessed historical colours.

These compact files support browsing, not conservation analysis or scholarly examination of fine surface detail. Technical decoding, stored hashes and HTTP delivery were checked for all fifteen. Visual spot checks covered *A Funeral*, both El Greco paintings and *An Egyptian Fellah Woman with her Baby*. That is not a claim of visual inspection of all fifteen or of browser-layout testing.
