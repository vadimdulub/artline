## Basis for the decisions

Photographic copyright and permission to reproduce public cultural property are separate issues. The Ministry's [national guidance, section 3.2.3](https://docs.italia.it/italia/icdp/icdp-pnd-circolazione-riuso-docs/it/v1.0-giugno-2022/acquisizione-circolazione-e-riuso-delle-riproduzioni-dei-beni-culturali-in-ambiente-digitale/tipologie-duso-delle-riproduzioni-di-beni-culturali.html) specifically addresses authorization and payment for images of public cultural property in paid commercial applications. A public app is not automatically a paid application. Artline's possible commercial use is therefore recorded as an unresolved clearance requirement where no suitable concession or documented exception has been established, rather than assuming a private-study exception.

The signed [DM 108 of 21 March 2024](https://asmo.cultura.gov.it/fileadmin/risorse/normativa/DM_21_marzo_2024_rep._108-signed.pdf) updates the Ministry's reproduction and fee guidelines. Its institutional scope must not be extended automatically to municipal collections or other public bodies. [Current Ministry practice](https://asmo.cultura.gov.it/servizi-al-pubblico/pubblicazione-e-concessione-duso-di-immagini), updated 14 May 2026, still distinguishes commercial concessions from personal or study use. That page supplies current context, not the fee schedule for the museums in this review. Monza also publishes its own [image concession conditions](https://www.museicivicimonza.it/servizi/concessione-immagini/). No fee has been calculated or paid.

The **49 holds** concern missing permission covering the intended use. They do not invalidate the photographers' licences or declare the underlying paintings copyrighted. Object-level ownership sections are preserved where present; custody and ownership are not conflated. In particular, the GAM Manzoni record names Pinacoteca di Brera and a historical deposit from the Academy; Castello's *Madonna Oggioni* records state ownership and a Brera deposit; the *Costantino* sheet records a deposit without resolving the owner in that section. These require the competent authority to be established before any concession is treated as sufficient. Several other files rely on established public institutional custody because no ownership text was already present in their image receipt.

The **five Poldi Pezzoli photographs remain**. Each exact work's SIRBeC catalogue sheet identifies private ownership, and each selected photograph was independently supplied by its photographer under CC BY 3.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0. The museum's [photographic supply contract](https://museopoldipezzoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Modulo-fototeca-ITA_vers-04201.pdf) concerns its own supplied material; this review does not extend that contract to these independently licensed photographs. This is a source-specific conclusion, not a blanket clearance for every Poldi image. The [per-image decisions](image-decisions.json) link the exact ownership and source evidence.

The **five Italian works held abroad remain** under their preserved museum releases: two Met CC0 images and three Vienna Academy CC BY 4.0 images. Their exact object, source, licence and credit evidence remains in the original receipts. [CC BY](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) and [CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) allow commercial reuse subject to their conditions. Credits, source links, change notices and applicable ShareAlike terms must remain available; the image licences must not be contradicted by additional restrictions in the app.
