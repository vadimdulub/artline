# Remaining Russian Museum title variants — 7 October 2026

**One WikiArt reproduction of Roerich’s Earth Paternoster was saved locally and attached to the existing review record Ж-1966.** Seventeen native museum references and twenty-eight unique source images were inspected across thirty source pages and thirty-six record-options. Sixteen records remain unresolved: fourteen have different pictured compositions; two sources were excluded from their metadata before image download. Catalogue dates, titles, media, dimensions, creator links, holdings and review/publication states are unchanged.

The [selected source](https://www.wikiart.org/en/nicholas-roerich/earth-paternoster-1907) explicitly dates the work to 1907 and labels it **Public domain**. That assertion is retained separately from the user’s WikiArt source approval. The 480 × 371 JPEG is **82,667 bytes**. Its [view qualification](view-scope-review.json) records tighter outer edges: some upper headgear, a far-left figure fragment and the native lower-right autograph are not retained. The complete supplied source frame and its brighter turquoise/gold colour are preserved without further cropping or recolouring.

WikiArt has no Original Title field on this page, and its selected untranslated index item has a null title. An [individual English-title review](english-title-review.json) pins the exact source page, index item, creator, native inventory and both photographs. No title or source date was invented and no general fallback was added. The exact rock-face motifs, plants, figures and crescent establish the physical version at more than 90% editorial confidence, which is not a calibrated probability.
