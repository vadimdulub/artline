# Selected local WikiArt recovery from earlier research — 6 October 2026

**66 images across 52 creators are saved locally and attached to existing local database records.** All 81 selected records were reviewed; 15 remain unchanged. This operation uses the user's explicit WikiArt approval in [ARTLINE_IMAGE_USE.md](../../ARTLINE_IMAGE_USE.md). It made no production connection and created no catalogue records, fixtures or publication changes.

Subsequent checkpoint: the [individual alternative-image review](../local-wikiart-version-correction-images-20261006/README.md) resolved the Matisse and Degas holds with different, independently inspected WikiArt photographs. Those two attachments belong to the follow-up operation; thirteen of this operation's fifteen held artworks remain unresolved. All rejected image files and the historical decisions below remain preserved.

The selection came from the [read-only audit of prior delivery research](../local-wikiart-prior-delivery-audit-20261006/README.md). Existing local titles, dates, creators, identifiers and holdings were compared to hash-pinned prior claims. Every selected WikiArt page was captured again and its object, creator, displayed date, image and actual rights labels checked. The local database retains its existing museum/Wikidata identifiers; WikiArt IDs were never substituted for them.

Seventy-nine reproductions reached visual review. Seventy-four source originals were reused after checking their original archive and download receipt; five were downloaded from the freshly selected WikiArt image URLs because only a prior derivative was available. Of the 66 attached images, 62 reuse verified originals and four use fresh source downloads. Every application image is a full-frame, proportionally resized JPEG of at most **99,523 bytes**. Originals remain separately archived.

All 79 final JPEGs were inspected. Fifty-five works were also compared visually against privately archived exact-object museum or authority-linked images. The individual decisions state whether they rely on that comparison or on a distinctive composition, creator, museum and pinned catalogue evidence. Earlier matching titles were not accepted over conflicting compositions. Source title/date variants remain explicit in attribution; existing catalogue metadata and unknown fields were preserved.

All 66 attached WikiArt pages assert general public-domain status. Their actual source labels, markup, credits and source links are retained separately from the user's approval. This records WikiArt's assertions and the authorized collection decision, without asserting independently obtained permission. Private museum comparison photographs were used for identity research and were not attached or copied into the application assets.
