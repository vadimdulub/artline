The [foundation](https://thf.gr/en/the-foundation/) identifies its permanent Papaloukas collection. Its [SearchCulture collection](https://www.searchculture.gr/aggregator/portal/collections/theocharakis?language=en) reported 2,567 items. Six bounded index pages supplied 180 leads: 17 already appeared in the historical delivery, one photograph documenting another artwork was excluded, and 162 original-work candidates were selected. The eighteenth historical record was added as a comparator, giving 180 individual records for detailed review. This is a selected pass, not a full archive download or a claim that all 2,567 records are eligible independent artworks.

Each selected/comparator record has both an individual SearchCulture page and the native foundation object page captured. All 180 agree on title, creator, medium, dimensions and creation-date text. Original source fields remain separate from SearchCulture enrichments. The source explicitly attributes these works to Spyros Papaloukas; linking that label to a production painter ID still requires live reconciliation. Source page IDs and image filenames have not been invented into accession numbers.

Related subjects are not automatically duplicates. The carol singers, Saint Demetrius studies, Archangel Michael studies and dolphin/medallion layouts were compared using drawing details, paper boundaries, materials and dimensions. Equal dimensions and adjacent page IDs alone do not establish a shared sheet. Multiple studies or motifs on a sheet count once. A study of an ancient object at Heraklion is a Papaloukas artwork held by Theocharakis, not a new ancient-object holding. Church and school names in titles are subjects or study destinations; current display is not inferred.
