# Leeds Art Gallery Cotmania additions — 9 October 2026

Added **49 artworks** from the museum-commissioned [Cotmania catalogue](https://cotmania.org/). Leeds Art Gallery increased from **88 to 137 linked records**, including **111 with eligible pre-1971 creation dates**. Every addition remains in review. The gallery has passed the 100 minimum; 89 more eligible works are needed for 200.

The selection contains 39 watercolours and 10 drawings, subject to exact source classification counts in the reviewed plan. Fifty physical objects were reviewed. One, *Cottage on Yarmouth Denes*, is explicitly scheduled as missing since a December1973 audit and was excluded from accepted holdings. Archive pages were not counted as artworks. Public search metadata was bounded to selected leads; no exhaustive catalogue or image download took place.

Eight creator labels were explicitly qualified using the catalogue essays: Cooke instead of the old composite attribution, pupil drawings on three reverse sides, uncertain Thirtle authorship, a Cotman-circle lake scene and two works whose Cotman attribution the curator rejects. A separate tree study already retained its unidentified-pupil label. Munn and John Joseph Cotman remain distinct creators. Paired recto/verso images count as one sheet; two former Spanish Arquebusier titles describe separate accessioned musketeer drawings with different poses and reverses. Source model/engraving dates were not substituted for creation dates. No painter links were guessed.

The [review](editorial-reviewed-002.json.gz), [plan](leeds-cotmania-additions-001-plan-002.json.gz) and [transaction receipt](leeds-cotmania-additions-001-applied.json) retain exact object URLs, native fields, provenance, qualified attributions and physical-version decisions. Fresh bounded identity checks covered **381 artworks and 841 citations**.54 native capture checks (53 unique bodies) and29 available comparison bodies were checksum-verified;12 older comparison bodies could not be reconstructed and are not presented as fresh confirmations. Numeric source-ID collisions from other collections were rejected as matches.

**22 offline checks, atomic database readback and zero-write replay passed.** All13,278 previous campaign records and381 scoped existing records were preserved. The first attempted transaction rolled back completely on an unsupported object-form value; the corrected plan uses the existing watercolor/drawing work types and leaves the icon-only object-form field empty. The failed attempt and correction remain [auditable](rolled-back-attempt-001.json). No schema changes, images, publication or current-display claims were added.
