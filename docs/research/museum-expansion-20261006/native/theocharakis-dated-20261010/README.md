The foundation's [SearchCulture collection](https://www.searchculture.gr/aggregator/portal/collections/theocharakis?language=en) provides an observed date-sort control. Its public GET form and response selection were checked before capturing three date-descending index pages, covering 90 cards. One record had already been reviewed, 29 new leads were deferred, and 60 were selected: all 47 index-labelled paintings plus 13 studies. Each selected work has both its SearchCulture object page and [native foundation object page](https://exhibition.thfdigital.gr/) captured. The two passes inspect 269 distinct index cards in total; they do not constitute a full download or review of the 2,567-record collection.

The 59 candidates comprise 28 oil paintings, 11 watercolours and 20 drawings in pencil, charcoal, crayon, pastel or mixed drawing media. The provider's collection categories remain literal evidence; a crayon drawing does not become an oil painting because its index category says “Painting.” All candidates have explicit creation dates or ranges between 1946 and 1956. They include Hydra and Paros landscapes, portraits, still lifes and original studies. Depicted places and objects do not establish other museum holdings or current display.

Two source dates differ materially. The native [Spyros Marinatos portrait](https://exhibition.thfdigital.gr/projects/103157/) gives 1950–1955 where SearchCulture gives 1950. The native [Danae portrait 103068](https://exhibition.thfdigital.gr/projects/103068/) gives 1948–1949 where SearchCulture gives 1948. The proposed records retain the broader native intervals and preserve both source values. Two Hydra studies likewise retain 1955–1956 rather than the index abbreviation 1955. No existing database dates have been changed.

Forty aggregator creator labels omit the life dates present on the native pages; the explicit names identify the same Spyros Papaloukas. Twenty-six medium differences are reviewed Greek synonyms for watercolour or canvas, including one mixed-medium description. Both literal versions are retained. The live painter ID is still unresolved, and the artist's life dates have not been used as artwork dates. Page IDs and image filenames remain source identifiers, not invented accessions.

Record 111796 remains a candidate; 111641 is not counted independently or accepted as a resolved metadata alias. Its true object or mistaken image remains uncertain. Equal dimensions with another nude study do not justify swapping images.
