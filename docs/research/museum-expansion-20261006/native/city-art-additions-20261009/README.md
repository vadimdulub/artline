# City Art Centre additions — 9 October 2026

Added **14 source-backed artworks in review** and linked **one existing John Duncan painting** to the museum. City Art Centre now has **103 linked works**, up from 88. Its minimum of 100 linked records is met; 97 more are needed for 200.

**11 additions have supported pre-1971 dates; three remain undated.** The reconciled Duncan is dated 1912. Date-eligible works increased from **29 to 41**. Undated records are not counted as date-eligible.

Sources include the [council’s portrait collection article](https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/news/article/14283/unmasked-exploring-scottish-portraiture-at-the-city-art-centre), [recent gifts](https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/news/article/14491/city-art-centre-showcases-most-recent-additions-to-fine-art-collection), curator Helen Scott’s collection articles in the [2014](https://ssahistory.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/ssah_newsletter_08-14.pdf) and [2022 newsletters](https://ssahistory.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/ssah-spring-summer2022-newsletter.pdf), the [University of Edinburgh’s exact lender list](https://www.trg.ed.ac.uk/exhibition/wide-new-kingdom-celtic-revival-scotland), and selected Art Fund acquisition records. [The decision ledger](editorial-reviewed-001.json.gz) preserves each title, maker, date, source and limitation.

The existing *Tristan and Isolde* record was linked using the 1912 *Tristan and Iseult* lender entry, its original WikiArt evidence and its existing image. Its metadata, image and review status were preserved. Six candidates remain held: Ramsay’s Katherine Hall portrait, Cameron’s *A Garment of War*, Paolozzi’s bronze *Horse’s Head*, Pow’s *Fallen Angels*, Robb’s *Cool House* and Blackadder’s *Irises*. Versions, cast dates and possible post-1970 creation require further evidence.

Three newsletters were retained; six pages were rendered and visually inspected to distinguish captions and neighbouring articles. Ten existing catalogue images and one selected 300-pixel research thumbnail supported object comparisons. The winter 2014 article has no named byline; it is not described as curator-authored. No catalogue images were attached. A National Galleries of Scotland request returned HTTP 403 and further host requests stopped. Three historical council URLs returned 404; their cached search snippets were not promoted into fresh evidence.

**22 offline checks, atomic readback and zero-write replay passed.** All 449 scoped existing records were preserved apart from the one authorized museum link and its evidence; all **13,910 prior campaign records** were unchanged. The bounded identity pool held 2,715 artworks and 6,121 citations. The context pass reconstructed 153 raw bodies; 45 citation bodies remained unavailable there, with the Duncan raw body subsequently recovered in the separate physical-object review. No publication, current-display or ownership claims were added.
