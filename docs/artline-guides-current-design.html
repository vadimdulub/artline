    Begin with what you can see

    In Anna Ancher’s blue room, sunlight forms a bright patch against the wall. In Krøyer’s beach scene, pale dresses stand against the blue shore. In Monet’s garden, look between the bridge, the plants and their reflections.

    Where does your eye go first? Use the filters below to compare the three paintings, or choose one artist to give a single work your full attention.

    Return to one detail

    Follow the brightest area into the colors beside it. Does the boundary stay sharp, or does it soften? Describe the difference before deciding what it means.

    The three works are examples for looking closely. Their shared decade gives us a starting point for comparison; it does not by itself establish influence between the artists.
